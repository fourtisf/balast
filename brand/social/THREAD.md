## Replies worth having ready

- **"What's the APY?"**

  > We don't show APY. The builder shows your share of the fees the pool actually paid today, with the arithmetic on screen. Past fees, not a promise.

- **"Do I need an NFT?"**

  > No. Staking creates it. It's Uniswap's receipt for your position, and it lives in your wallet.

- **"Is there a token / CA?"**

  > No token. If there ever is one, the address goes on balast.xyz first. Anything else is not ours.
